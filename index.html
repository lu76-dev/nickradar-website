// the idea
real life first. digital second.

You meet interesting people every day — on the train, in a café, at a concert. But there's no respectful, low-pressure way to stay in touch.

nickradar solves exactly that. You carry your nickname on a sticker — on your jacket, bag, or a card. Someone sees it, searches for it in the app, and sends you a request. Anonymous. No pressure. You decide.

No swiping. No algorithm. The encounter happens in real life first — then in the app.

// how it works
simple as that.
01
You carry your nickname — on a sticker on your jacket, bag, t-shirt or card. Your nick is your identity in the real world.
02
Someone sees your nickname, opens nickradar, searches for it and sends you an anonymous request with a short message.
03
You say yes or no. Both say yes → 48h chat opens. Chat, meet, connect. After 48h it's gone. Ephemeral by design.
// key principles
what makes nickradar different.
no algorithm. no one gets pushed to you. the encounter happens in real life first.
anonymous first. the requester is anonymous until both say yes.
equal rights. both seeker and target decide. no power imbalance.
ephemeral. 48h chat, then it's gone. no digital clutter. real meetings only.
privacy first. no profile browsing, no followers, no feed. just nicknames.
pay per match. no subscription. you only pay if both say yes. €3 / €1.50.
